關於中心About

健康資訊科技國際研究中心（ICHIT）是臺北醫學大學醫學科技學院設立的院級研究中心，為校內正式研究單位。中心於 2014 年由李友專特聘教授創立，是台灣首個專注於健康資訊科技發展與國際合作的研究中心。The International Center for Health Information Technology (ICHIT) is a college-level research center of the College of Medical Science and Technology at Taipei Medical University and a formal research unit of the university. Founded in 2014 by Distinguished Professor Yu-Chuan (Jack) Li, it was Taiwan's first research center dedicated to health information technology and international collaboration.

中心結合教學醫院與轉譯醫學經驗，推動「早覺醫療」（Earlier Medicine）模式，以數據與人工智慧實現疾病的早期預測、診斷與介入。團隊由生物醫學資訊、資料探勘、自然語言處理專家，以及神經、心臟、腎臟、皮膚、急診等臨床醫師組成。Working with TMU's teaching hospitals, the center advances the Earlier Medicine model: using data and AI for earlier prediction, diagnosis and intervention. The team brings together experts in biomedical informatics, data mining and natural language processing with clinicians in neurology, cardiology, nephrology, dermatology and emergency medicine.

產學轉譯與新創Translation and spin-offs

ICHIT 的研究成果透過技術移轉與衍生新創走向市場；並支持成立數位健康新創轉譯產學聯盟（DHT），把北醫體系的臨床與驗證資源開放給產業。ICHIT brings research to market through technology transfer and spin-offs, and supports the Digital Health Translation Alliance (DHT), which opens TMU's clinical and validation resources to industry.
